Rationale & migration

What's in this project:

  • Echo Design System: tokens (:root, dark, system, reduced motion), the Tailwind v4 @theme block, contrast table, component sheet
  • Home · Detail & Capture · Library & Collections · Revisits & Search · Settings & Public, each with a light/dark tweak
  • Echo Directions: the three Step 1 options

Rationale

Palette

Cream paper and warm brown-black ink replace white and gray, so the app reads like a notebook instead of a dashboard. The one accent is iron-gall blue, the colour of a fountain pen. It's quiet enough to sit next to long passages, it's clearly different from the error red, and it never takes over the page. The collection colours (moss, ochre, heather and a ringed neutral) come from the same paper family. They show up as small marks and pale panels, never as large fills. Dark mode is the same desk at night: warm umber surfaces, not blue-gray. Every pair is computed in the contrast table and passes AA in both themes.

Type

EB Garamond carries every quote. It's an old-style book face with real warmth at 19px and genuine presence at 48px. Hanken Grotesk is a calm, slightly humanist sans with open apertures, and it handles everything else, including the wordmark. The pairing works because the two never compete: the serif is always the largest and only literary voice on the screen. The one place the serif appears without being a saved quote is the landing tagline, which the brief asks for.

Shape

The corners are softened like cut card stock: 6px for controls, 10px for panels, 4px for chips. Pills and the 14–32px radii are gone, so nothing looks like a mobile app's bubble UI. Panels are separated by one warm hairline and a change in surface tone, not by shadow. The single shadow tier is kept for things that genuinely float: dialogs, menus, toasts and the raised Add button. Cards respond to hover with a darker border, not a lift. A 3px CSS dot pattern (one gradient, no image) adds a trace of paper texture to the page background.

Motion

Three durations (150, 200 and 250ms) and two curves, used only where something changes meaning: the Echo swap, dialogs, toasts and hover. The swap is a short fade and rise with a height ease, so a long passage never makes the page jump. With reduced motion, everything becomes a 150ms opacity cross-fade: still a signal, no movement.

Today's Echo as the bold moment

It's the only place in the app where type reaches 44px, the only panel with 56px of padding, and the only block that splits into a quote column and a “You wrote” margin note on desktop, like a reader's annotation beside a printed page. It sits directly under a plain greeting and above a deliberately muted grid, so nothing competes with it. “Echo me something” is the screen's only filled button. Everything else on Home is smaller, quieter, and capped.

Migration · components

Style only (swap tokens, same markup)

Buttons (all five), text input, textarea, select, TagInput, CollectionPicker, RevisitPicker, Chip/Tag, AccentDot, FavoriteButton, Dialog, Dropdown, Pagination, Skeleton, Spinner, EmptyState, ErrorState, SkipLink, Sidebar, Rail, mobile header.

Small structural changes

  • Today's Echo: a two-column grid on desktop (≥1128px), with “You wrote” as a side note; it stacks below that.
  • QuoteCard: a footer row separated by a hairline holds the collection (dot and name) and the heart. Tags sit above it.
  • CollectionCard: a 4px accent bar along the top edge, with the name always visible beside it.
  • Library grid: CSS columns masonry instead of a fixed grid, so short and long quotes pack without gaps.
  • Toast: inverted (ink background, canvas text) instead of a white card.
  • Tabs: the active tab is marked with a 2px ink underline (inset shadow) instead of a filled background.
  • Bottom tab bar: the active item gets a neutral pill behind its icon.
  • Quote textarea: uses the quote serif (font-quote).
  • Search: matches are highlighted with a <mark> on the ochre tint, with an underline so the highlight isn't colour alone.

Please confirm (possible product changes)

  • Upcoming Revisits rows show Change and Cancel, the same actions as on Echo detail.
  • The Library mobile filter shows 3 tags plus “More tags”, rather than every tag.
  • The Quick Capture footer shows a hint, “Only the words are needed.”, and Save stays disabled until there's a quote.
  • Settings uses an in-page section list (Account, Appearance, Privacy, Data) on desktop.
